My Poet Autograph

Upload your photo, hand your phone to the performer, and have them sign right on the image. Your keepsake will include both official logos.

On a phone, Take a Picture opens the camera (where supported). Choose Existing Photo opens your photo library. On a computer, the camera option may open a file picker instead.

Your photograph will appear here after upload.

The logos are automatically added to the exported photo. You can sign anywhere except where the logos appear. No photo is uploaded to a server in this demo.
